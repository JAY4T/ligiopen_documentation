# Workflow: Prepare Match Week Operations

## Goal

Ensure each match week runs smoothly despite real-world pressure.

## Checklist

- venue readiness recheck
- weather and transport risk scan
- officiating availability confirmation
- club communication and timing alignment
- public information consistency

## Kenyan operations tip

Treat weather and travel as first-class risks, not side notes.
