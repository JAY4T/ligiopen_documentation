# Workflow: Close a Season with Confidence

## Goal

Conclude the season with trusted outcomes and clear transition planning.

## Steps

1. confirm final validated standings and progression outcomes
2. publish official end-of-season summary
3. document major lessons from operations
4. prepare transition actions for next season planning

## Kenyan context tip

Public communication quality at season close shapes acceptance for the next season kickoff.
