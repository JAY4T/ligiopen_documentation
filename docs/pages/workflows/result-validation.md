# Workflow: Validate Results and Resolve Disputes

## Goal

Keep standings trusted by ensuring result quality and fair review.

## Steps

1. Validate submitted result record quality.
2. Confirm any dispute flags quickly.
3. Run structured review where needed.
4. Publish final decision and rationale.
5. Keep progression communication clear.

## Why this works

Fast, structured review protects confidence in title and progression outcomes.
