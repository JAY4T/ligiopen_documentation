# Workflow: Handle Postponements with Control

## Goal

Move from disruption to a fair new schedule without confusion.

## Steps

1. Capture the postponement reason clearly.
2. Coordinate affected roles quickly.
3. Select realistic alternative timing.
4. Recheck venue and competition integrity.
5. Publish updates early to clubs and fans.

## Outcome to protect

Transparency. People can accept bad weather; they struggle with silent decisions.
