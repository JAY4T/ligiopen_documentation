# Workflow: Launch a New Season

## Goal

Move from planning to a competition-ready season with confidence.

## Steps

1. Confirm season window and governance owners.
2. Prepare league and division structure.
3. Confirm club readiness deadlines.
4. Validate venue and officiating assumptions.
5. Publish opening communication to all stakeholders.

## Success signs

- no ambiguity on start dates
- participation expectations are clear
- operations teams are aligned before first fixture release
