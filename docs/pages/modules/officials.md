# Officials Module: Reliability Under Pressure

Football fairness depends on match officials being prepared, available, and properly assigned.

This module supports that foundation.

## What it covers

- official role readiness
- assignment visibility
- conflict awareness in scheduling

## Why this module is essential

When official planning is weak:

- match legitimacy is questioned
- result disputes increase
- league credibility suffers

When official planning is strong:

- match flow is cleaner
- decisions are easier to defend
- competition trust improves

## Kenyan context

Officials may face travel and scheduling constraints across counties.

Clear assignment planning lowers no-show risk and helps matchday recovery when disruptions happen.

## Leadership tip

Build officiating depth. One assignment plan is good; a backup plan is better.
