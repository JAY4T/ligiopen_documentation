# Fixture Module: Turning Plans into Match Reality

Fixtures are where governance becomes real.

This module controls the match calendar and protects order when disruption arrives.

## What this module handles

- fixture planning and release
- match progression state (planned to completed)
- postponement and rescheduling flow
- operational conflict awareness

## Why fixture quality defines season quality

If fixture management is weak:

- clubs lose planning confidence
- officials are stretched unpredictably
- fans stop trusting published schedules

If fixture management is strong:

- match weeks become predictable
- disruptions are absorbed with less friction
- standings progression stays credible

## Kenyan pressure points this module must absorb

- rain disruption in key grounds
- urban venue sharing across events
- long-distance travel constraints
- derby security and timing sensitivity

## Operational story

When a major fixture changes late, this module should answer three questions fast:

1. What changed?
2. Who is affected?
3. What is the approved recovery path?

If those answers are clear, even difficult weeks remain manageable.

## Good practice

Publish only what you can defend operationally.

Use flexibility, but never hide decisions.
