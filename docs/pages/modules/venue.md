# Venue Module: Operational Reality on the Ground

Venues define whether football plans can actually happen.

This module keeps venue readiness visible and manageable.

## What it helps manage

- venue usability context
- schedule pressure around blackout periods
- practical conditions affecting match viability

## Why venue discipline matters

A fixture is only real when venue conditions, timing, and safety are realistic.

Ignoring venue realities creates cancellation chains and credibility damage.

## Kenyan reality examples

- heavy rain affecting pitch quality
- shared public venues with competing events
- accessibility and crowd-flow constraints
- late infrastructure issues

## Good operational habit

Treat venue checks as continuous, not one-time.

The best leagues validate venue readiness repeatedly before high-stakes weeks.
