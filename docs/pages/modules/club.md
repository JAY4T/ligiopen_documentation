# Club Module: The Institution Layer of Football

Clubs are the long-term institutions that carry culture, supporters, and continuity.

This module keeps club identity and team structures organized across seasons.

## What this module enables

- clean club records
- team identity under each club
- clear club-side administrative ownership

## Why this matters

When club records are weak:

- registrations become slow and contested
- team identity can be unclear
- league operations spend time on avoidable verification

When club records are strong:

- onboarding is faster
- communication is cleaner
- season transitions are smoother

## Kenyan context

Many clubs represent deep local identity.

Keeping club structure clean helps protect that identity while improving national competition operations.

## Club admin reality

A good club admin behaves like an operations manager:

- prepares early
- communicates quickly
- keeps roster and readiness evidence current

This reduces last-minute conflict with league operations.
