# League Module: Structuring a Fair Season

The League Module is where order begins.

It defines the football landscape for the season and ensures clubs enter the right structure before competition starts.

## What this module solves

- confusion around season context
- inconsistent participation handling
- weak progression traceability

## Season, league, division in plain terms

- **Season**: the yearly chapter (for example 2026/27)
- **League**: the competition identity over many years
- **Division**: the active playing table inside that season

This distinction helps leagues avoid mixing records across years.

## Team progression entry point

Before any club can influence standings, team participation must be reviewed and confirmed.

This ensures:

- legitimacy of entrants
- balanced division composition
- cleaner fixture generation

## Why this matters for stakeholders

For league leaders: governance becomes consistent.

For clubs: expectations are clear and defendable.

For fans: the table reflects proper competition setup.

## Kenyan story snapshot

A county competition once suffered yearly disputes because entry rules changed informally each season.

With structured participation flow, teams could no longer claim hidden favoritism.

The result: less politics, better football focus.

## Good practice

Treat league setup as governance work, not data entry.

If this module is strong, downstream modules perform better.
