# Standings Module: The Public Truth of Performance

Standings are not just numbers. They are a public contract.

They tell clubs and fans whether the competition has been run fairly.

## What this module protects

- transparent ranking movement
- consistent tie separation logic
- reliable progression interpretation

## What people read in standings

- who is leading
- who is chasing
- who is at risk
- how momentum is changing

## Why recalculation discipline matters

When outcomes are corrected or disputed, table trust can break quickly.

A disciplined standings process ensures updates feel fair and traceable, not arbitrary.

## Kenyan context story

Late-season title races in Kenya can be separated by tiny margins.

In such moments, clear ranking logic prevents unnecessary controversy and keeps focus on football.

## Good communication habit

When standings change after review decisions, explain the reason in plain language.

Transparency is part of competition integrity.
