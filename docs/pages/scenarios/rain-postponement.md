# Scenario 1: Rain, Water, and a Tough Matchday Decision

## Story

A major Nairobi fixture is set for Saturday afternoon. Overnight rain floods key sections of the pitch.

By morning, safety is at risk.

## How LigiOpen helps

- decision is captured early
- postponement is recorded cleanly
- rescheduling is coordinated with venue and competition flow

## Why this matters

This protects players, officials, and public trust.

In Kenya's rainy windows, this kind of clarity can save an entire match week from confusion.
