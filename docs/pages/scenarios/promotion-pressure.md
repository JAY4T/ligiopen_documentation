# Scenario 8: Promotion and Survival Pressure

## Story

Two clubs are tied near season end. One chases promotion, the other fights to avoid the drop.

## How LigiOpen helps

- table movement stays transparent
- progression consequences are visible each round
- final outcomes are easier to communicate and defend

## Why this matters

The final weeks of a Kenyan season can be intense. Transparent progression rules reduce conflict.
