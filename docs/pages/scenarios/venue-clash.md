# Scenario 2: Last-Minute Venue Clash

## Story

A match must move because a stadium becomes unavailable due to a state function.

The replacement venue already has another football event nearby in time.

## How LigiOpen helps

- flags the operational clash
- forces planners to choose better spacing or controlled override
- keeps change accountability visible

## Why this matters

Late venue shifts can destabilize clubs and supporters.

Structured handling protects fairness and communication quality.
