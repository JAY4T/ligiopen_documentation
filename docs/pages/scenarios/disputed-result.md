# Scenario 4: Disputed Result After a Big Match

## Story

A losing club challenges a decisive match incident. Public pressure rises quickly on radio and social media.

## How LigiOpen helps

- dispute status keeps the issue visible
- review process stays structured
- validated outcome returns clarity to standings

## Why this matters

Disputes are not only technical issues; they are trust issues. Structured review protects league credibility.
