# Scenario 3: Sudden Player Availability Changes

## Story

A club loses two starters before a high-pressure game: one injury and one national duty call-up.

## How LigiOpen helps

- availability status is visible before matchday
- coaches can adapt selection plans early
- communication remains clear between club operations and competition officials

## Why this matters

Availability transparency keeps football quality high and reduces avoidable lineup disputes.
