# Scenario 6: Official Fails to Report

## Story

On matchday, a key official is delayed and cannot arrive on time.

## How LigiOpen helps

- assignment visibility helps emergency coordination
- replacement decisions are traceable
- match governance can continue under controlled recovery

## Why this matters

No-show situations are operational stress tests. Prepared fallback flow prevents total disruption.
