# Scenario 7: From Group Battles to Knockout Drama

## Story

A club starts in a balanced group, survives a tense final group match, then advances into knockout football.

## How LigiOpen helps

- progression path is clear
- stage transitions are visible to all stakeholders
- bracket movement is easy to understand publicly

## Why this matters

Fans and clubs need a clear path from first group game to final. This story makes that journey understandable.
