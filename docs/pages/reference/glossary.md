# Glossary (Plain Language)

## Core football terms

- **Fixture**: A planned match.
- **Round-robin**: Everyone plays everyone (once or home/away).
- **Knockout**: Lose and exit progression path.
- **Derby**: High-rivalry match.
- **Aggregate score**: Combined score across two legs.

## Competition terms

- **Season**: The football calendar period.
- **League**: The long-term competition identity.
- **Division**: The active table for a season.
- **Group stage**: Mini-league phase in tournaments.
- **Bracket**: Knockout progression map.

## Operations terms

- **Postponement**: A match moved due to conditions.
- **Reschedule**: New approved timing for a moved match.
- **Dispute**: Formal challenge to a match outcome.
- **Availability**: Whether a player or official can participate.
- **Blackout date**: Time a venue is not usable.

## Role terms

- **Super Admin**: System governance owner.
- **League Admin**: Competition operator.
- **Club Admin**: Club-side operations manager.
- **Content Admin**: Match record operations support.
- **Official**: Referee and match governance professional.
