# Frequently Asked Questions

## Who is this documentation for?

League admins, club admins, operations teams, sports stakeholders, and anyone who needs a plain-language understanding of how the system runs competitions.

## Is LigiOpen only for football?

LigiOpen is football-first today, but many of its operating concepts are broad enough for other league formats when rules are mapped properly.

## Can this documentation help us evaluate non-football rollout?

Yes. Use [Multi-League Support](/multi-league-support/) plus the modules and workflow pages to evaluate fit and caveats.

## Why so much scenario content?

Because competition operations are easiest to understand through realistic stories and decision paths.

## Can fans use this documentation too?

Yes. It helps fans understand progression, standings movement, and why schedule changes happen.
