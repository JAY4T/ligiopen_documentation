# Risks and Edge Cases (Layperson View)

## Common operational risks

- late venue withdrawal
- weather-driven schedule compression
- official unavailability close to kickoff
- disputed outcomes under media pressure
- fixture congestion during key season windows

## What good governance looks like

- decisions are captured and visible
- role ownership is clear
- updates are communicated early
- recovery paths are practical and fair

## Kenyan context emphasis

In local football operations, the difference between a smooth week and a chaotic one is often communication speed plus realistic scheduling.
