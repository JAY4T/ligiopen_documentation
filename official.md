# Official / Referee Guide 🟡

As an **Official** on LigiOpen, you're a registered referee or assistant referee. League Admins assign you to matches, you can accept or reject those assignments, set your availability, and on match day, record events for the fixtures you're officiating.

---

## What is the Official Role?

Officials are the match day referees and assistant referees who make it all happen on the pitch. On LigiOpen, your profile stores your certification, your availability, your assignment history, and your preferences — so League Admins can find the right official for each match.

> 💡 **The dedicated Official section of the app is coming soon.** Some features described here are currently accessed through your Profile tab. A dedicated Officials tab with a full dashboard is planned for an upcoming release.

---

## How Do I Get the Official Role?

You don't create your official profile yourself. Here's how it works:

1. A **Super Admin** creates your official profile on LigiOpen — entering your name, email, phone, county, certification level, and primary role (Center Referee, AR1, AR2, or Fourth Official).
2. Once your profile is created, your LigiOpen user account (if you have one) is linked to it.
3. The **Official** role is added to your account.

> ✅ **If you don't have a LigiOpen account yet:** Download the app, register with the same email the Super Admin used for your profile, and your account will link automatically.

## Receiving Assignment Notifications

When a League Admin assigns you to a match, you'll receive a **notification** on your phone.

To view your assignments:
1. Open LigiOpen.
2. Go to your **Profile tab**.
3. Look for the **Assignments** section. (A dedicated Official tab is coming soon — this will become much easier.)

Each assignment shows:
- Home team vs Away team
- Date and time
- Venue
- Your role in this match (e.g. Center Referee)
- Current status (Pending / Accepted / Rejected)

---

## Accepting an Assignment

When you're available and happy to take the match:

1. Open the assignment.
2. Tap **Accept**.
3. The League Admin sees your confirmation. The match is now confirmed for you. ✅

---

## Rejecting an Assignment

If you can't make it:

1. Open the assignment.
2. Tap **Reject**.
3. Enter a **reason** (e.g. "Travelling that weekend", "Already assigned to another match", "Family commitment").
4. Tap **Confirm**.

The League Admin receives a notification and can assign a different official to the match.

> 💡 **Reject as early as possible** so the League Admin has time to find a replacement. Don't leave it to the last minute.

---

## Setting Your Availability

You can mark yourself as **unavailable** for a period of time — useful when you're injured, travelling, or otherwise unable to officiate.

1. Go to your **Profile tab**.
2. Tap **Official Profile**.
3. Tap the **Availability** section.
4. Toggle to **Unavailable**.
5. Add a note explaining why (e.g. "Knee injury — out until 15 May 2025", "Annual leave 1–15 August").

This is **advisory** — League Admins can see your availability status when browsing officials. They'll typically avoid assigning you during periods you've marked as unavailable.

> 💡 **Remember to toggle back to Available** once the period is over. Otherwise, League Admins may continue to skip you for assignments.

---

## Adding Constraints and Preferences

You can tell LigiOpen about specific preferences or limitations:

- "I prefer Nyayo National Stadium"
- "Avoid Moi Sports Centre (transport issues)"
- "Maximum 1 match per week"
- "Prefer Saturday afternoon slots"

To add a constraint:

1. Go to **Profile tab → Official Profile → Constraints**.
2. Tap **+**.
3. Enter your preference or limitation as a note.
4. Tap **Save**.

> 💡 **Constraints are advisory, not blocking.** They guide League Admins when making assignment decisions, but a League Admin can still assign you to any match regardless of your stated preferences. If you have a hard limitation, make sure to reject the assignment and explain why.

## On Match Day — Recording Events

If you are the **Center Referee** assigned to a match, you can also record match events directly in LigiOpen — the same way a Content Admin does.

This can be useful as a backup or when no Content Admin is assigned.

To record events:

1. Open the fixture from your assignments list.
2. The match controls work the same as the Content Admin interface:
   - Tap **Kick Off** when you blow the whistle
   - Tap **+** to record goals, cards, substitutions
   - Tap **Update Minute** to keep the clock current
   - Tap **Submit Result** at full time

> 💡 **In most matches, a dedicated Content Admin will handle this.** You'll only need to record events yourself if the Content Admin is absent or asks for your help.

For a detailed walkthrough of recording match events, see the [Content Admin Guide →]({{ '/content-admin' | relative_url }}).

## A Real Scenario: James Manages His Schedule

> **James Ochieng** is a FIFA-certified Center Referee based in Nairobi. He's been officiating Kenyan football for 8 years.
>
> On Monday morning, he gets a LigiOpen notification: "You've been assigned to Gor Mahia vs AFC Leopards — Saturday, Nyayo Stadium, 3:00 PM — Center Referee."
>
> He opens the app and goes to his Profile tab → Assignments. He can see the fixture details. He's free that Saturday, so he taps **Accept**. The League Admin sees the confirmation. ✅
>
> Later that week, he gets another notification: a second assignment for the same Saturday — AFC Leopards vs Tusker (a different match, he's been assigned to both by mistake). He opens that assignment, taps **Reject**, and writes "Already assigned to Gor Mahia vs AFC Leopards on this date."
>
> He also opens his **Availability** section and adds a note: "Unavailable 20–27 August — CAF referee training camp in Cairo." This way, no League Admin will assign him during that week without knowing.
>
> On Saturday, James arrives at Nyayo Stadium at 2:00 PM. The Content Admin, James Njoroge, is already there with the LigiOpen app ready. James focuses on refereeing. James Njoroge handles the live event recording. Both doing their jobs, the match goes smoothly. ⚽🟡
