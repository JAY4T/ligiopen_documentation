# Understanding Roles in LigiOpen 🎭

LigiOpen uses a **role-based system**. Every user starts as a Fan, and additional roles are assigned based on what you do in the world of sports.

The powerful thing? **You can have multiple roles at the same time.** A Club Admin is also a Fan. A player is also a Fan. You switch between your roles using the **Profile tab → Switch Role**.

---

## All Roles at a Glance

| Emoji | Role | Who has it | What they do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🙋 | **Fan** | Every LigiOpen user | Browse fixtures, standings, clubs, live updates |
| ⚽ | **Player** | Registered athletes | View player card, stats, declare availability, club history |
| 🛡️ | **Club Admin** | Club managers/officials | Manage club profile, register players, submit team registrations |
| 📋 | **League Admin** | Competition managers | Create divisions, generate fixtures, approve results, manage standings |
| 🔑 | **Super Admin** | Platform administrators | Full control — create clubs, leagues, seasons, assign all roles |
| 📹 | **Content Admin** | Match day operators | Record live goals, cards, subs; submit final result |
| 🟡 | **Official** | Referees and assistants | View assignments, accept/reject, set availability |

---

## Role Details

### 🙋 Fan

**Who has this role?**
Every single person who downloads LigiOpen. It's the default role — you get it automatically when you sign up.

**How do you get it?**
Automatically, when you create a LigiOpen account.

**What can you do?**
- Browse all fixtures (upcoming, live, completed)
- Check league standings for any division
- View club profiles and their teams
- Follow live match updates (goals, cards, subs in real time)
- View fixture details including match events and assigned officials

**What screens do you see?**
Five tabs: **Home**, **Fixtures**, **Standings**, **Clubs**, **Profile**

---

### ⚽ Player

**Who has this role?**
Any registered athlete who has claimed their player profile on LigiOpen.

**How do you get it?**
Your club admin registers you as a "shadow player" and sends you an email invitation. You tap the link in the email, and if you already have a LigiOpen account, your player profile is linked immediately. If you're new, you create an account first, then the profile is linked. The **Player** role is granted to you automatically after the link is made.

**What can you do?**
- See your personal player card (name, position, jersey number, team)
- View your current season stats (goals, assists, appearances, cards, etc.)
- See your complete stats history across all seasons
- Browse your team's upcoming fixtures and **declare your availability** (Available / Unavailable / Maybe)
- View your club history (every club you've played for)
- See your club's team standings across divisions

**What screens do you see?**
Five tabs: **My Profile**, **Fixtures**, **History**, **Standings**, **Profile**

---

### 🛡️ Club Admin

**Who has this role?**
The manager, secretary, or official representative of a registered club.

**How do you get it?**
A **Super Admin** assigns you the Club Admin role and links you to a specific club. You'll receive an email notification when this happens.

**What can you do?**
- Update your club profile (name, logo, colors, stadium, contact info)
- Create and manage teams within your club (e.g. First Team, Reserve Team, U-20)
- Register players as "shadow players" and invite them via email
- Edit player information and team assignments
- Release players (end their time at your club)
- Transfer players to other clubs
- Register your teams for league divisions
- View your squad's upcoming fixtures
- Set or manage player availability for fixtures

**What screens do you see?**
Four tabs: **Dashboard**, **Squad**, **Fixtures**, **Club**, **Profile**

---

### 📋 League Admin

**Who has this role?**
The competition administrator — the person who runs a league or tournament on LigiOpen.

**How do you get it?**
A **Super Admin** assigns you the League Admin role and links you to a specific league.

**What can you do?**
- Create and manage divisions within your league (e.g. "Premier Division 2025/26", "Division 2")
- Review and approve/reject team registration requests from clubs
- Generate the full fixture schedule with one tap (round-robin, weekends only, etc.)
- Publish fixtures so fans can see them
- Assign Content Admins and match officials to specific fixtures
- Postpone or reschedule fixtures
- Approve or dispute match results submitted by Content Admins
- Manage standings (and force-recalculate if needed)
- Create and manage venues, including time slots and blackout dates

**What screens do you see?**
Six tabs: **Dashboard**, **Divisions**, **Fixtures**, **Registrations**, **Standings**, **Venues**, **Profile**

---

### 🔑 Super Admin

**Who has this role?**
The highest-level administrators of the LigiOpen platform — typically the organisation running LigiOpen or a trusted national sports body.

**How do you get it?**
Assigned directly by the LigiOpen team. There are very few Super Admins.

**What can you do?**
Everything a League Admin can do, plus:
- Create clubs and leagues from scratch
- Create seasons and set the current active season
- Assign Club Admins to clubs
- Assign League Admins to leagues
- Manage all user roles (grant or remove any role from any user)
- Create official (referee) profiles
- Delete venues

**What screens do you see?**
A side-drawer navigation with: **Users**, **Officials**, **Seasons**, **Venues**, **Leagues**, **Clubs**, **Profile**

---

### 📹 Content Admin

**Who has this role?**
The person at the stadium on match day responsible for recording everything that happens.

**How do you get it?**
A **Super Admin** assigns you the Content Admin role. Then, a **League Admin** assigns you to specific fixtures. You'll only see fixtures you've been assigned to.

**What can you do?**
- See all your assigned fixtures in your dashboard
- Kick off a match (moves it from Scheduled → In Progress, starts the live clock)
- Record live match events: goals, assists, yellow cards, red cards, substitutions
- Update the match clock throughout the game
- Submit the final result (home score, away score, attendance)

**What screens do you see?**
Two tabs: **Dashboard**, **Profile**

---

### 🟡 Official

**Who has this role?**
Registered referees and assistant referees.

**How do you get it?**
A **Super Admin** creates your official profile (name, certification level, role, county). Your LigiOpen account is then linked to that official profile.

**What can you do?**
- View your upcoming match assignments
- Accept or reject assignment requests
- Set your availability (e.g. "unavailable 10–20 May — travelling")
- Add constraints/preferences (e.g. "prefer Nyayo Stadium")
- Record match events for fixtures you're assigned to (same as Content Admin)

> 💡 **Note:** The dedicated Official section of the app is coming soon. Some features are currently managed through your Profile tab.

## Having Multiple Roles

You can absolutely have more than one role. This is very common:

- A **Club Admin** is always also a **Fan** — they can still browse fixtures and standings like any supporter.
- A **Player** at a club might also be a **Club Admin** for a different club (e.g. a playing coach).
- A **Content Admin** might also be a **Fan** and a **Player** at their own club.

**Your Profile tab shows all your roles.** Switch between them anytime:

1. Tap **Profile** tab (bottom right or drawer).
2. Scroll to **Switch Role**.
3. Tap the role you want.

The app reloads the tabs and screens for that role.

---

## Role-Switching: A Real Example

> **Coach Mwangi** is the team manager of Sofapaka FC. He has two LigiOpen roles: **Fan** and **Club Admin**.
>
> In the morning, he opens LigiOpen and switches to his **Club Admin** role. He checks the squad — a new player joined yesterday. He registers the player, fills in their details, and sends them an invite email.
>
> In the afternoon, he switches back to his **Fan** role, and watches the Gor Mahia vs AFC Leopards live fixture on the app, cheering from his couch. Same phone, same app, two completely different experiences.
