# Getting Started with LigiOpen 🚀

Welcome! This guide walks you through everything you need to do the first time you open LigiOpen — from downloading the app to exploring your first fixtures.

---

## Step 1 — Download the App 📲

Open the **Google Play Store** on your Android phone and search for **"LigiOpen"**. Tap **Install**.

> 💡 **iOS (iPhone) coming soon.** For now, LigiOpen is available on Android only.

---

## Step 2 — Create Your Account

When you open LigiOpen for the first time, you'll see the welcome screen. Tap **Register** (not Log In).

Fill in the following details:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Full Name** | Your real name (e.g. "James Kamau") |
| **Email** | A valid email address you check regularly |
| **Phone Number** | Your Kenyan or international mobile number |
| **Password** | At least 6 characters |

Tap **Create Account** when you're done.

> ✅ **Tip:** Use the same email address your club admin would use to invite you as a player. This makes it easy for your club to find you when they register you.

> ⚠️ **Important:** Your email address must be unique — you can't use an email that's already registered on LigiOpen.

---

## Step 3 — Verify Your Email ✉️

After registering, LigiOpen sends a **6-digit verification code** to your email address.

1. Open your email app and find the message from LigiOpen.
2. Copy the 6-digit code.
3. Go back to LigiOpen and type the code into the verification screen.
4. Tap **Verify**.

> ⚠️ **You won't be able to log in until you verify your email.** This step protects your account and makes sure the email address is really yours.

> 💡 **Didn't receive the code?** Check your spam/junk folder. If it's not there after a few minutes, tap **Resend Code** in the app.

---

## Step 4 — Your First Login

Once your email is verified, LigiOpen logs you in automatically and takes you straight to your **Fan dashboard**.

You'll see the **5 main tabs** at the bottom of the screen:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| 🏠 **Home** | Recent and live fixtures |
| 📅 **Fixtures** | All upcoming and past fixtures |
| 🏆 **Standings** | League tables |
| 🛡️ **Clubs** | Browse all clubs |
| 👤 **Profile** | Your account and settings |

---

## Step 5 — Explore as a Fan

As a Fan, you can browse everything publicly available on LigiOpen right away — no extra setup needed.

Here's what you can do:

- **Home tab** → See live matches and recent fixtures from all competitions.
- **Fixtures tab** → Filter fixtures by division or status. Tap any fixture to see the full details and match events.
- **Standings tab** → Pick a league and division to see the league table with points, goal difference, and form.
- **Clubs tab** → Browse all registered clubs. Tap any club to see its profile and teams.

> 💡 **No subscription needed.** Browsing LigiOpen as a Fan is completely free.

---

## Step 6 — Getting Additional Roles

Starting out, you're a **Fan**. Other roles unlock more features:

| Role | How you get it |
|---|---|
| **Player** | Your club admin registers you and sends you an email invite to claim your player profile |
| **Club Admin** | A Super Admin assigns this to you |
| **League Admin** | A Super Admin assigns this to you |
| **Content Admin** | A Super Admin assigns this to you |
| **Official** | A Super Admin creates your official profile |

You don't request roles yourself — they're assigned to you by the people who manage your league or club.

👉 **[Learn about all roles in detail →]({{ '/user-roles' | relative_url }})**

---

## Step 7 — Switching Between Roles

If you have more than one role, you can switch between them at any time:

1. Tap the **Profile tab** (bottom right).
2. Scroll down to the **Switch Role** section.
3. Tap the role you want to switch to.

The app reloads with the tabs and features for that role.

> 💡 **Example:** If you're both a Club Admin and a Fan, you'll see two roles listed. Tap "Club Admin" to manage your club, or tap "Fan" to browse fixtures like a normal user.

---

## Forgot Your Password?

No problem! Here's how to reset it:

1. On the login screen, tap **Forgot Password?**
2. Enter your registered email address. Tap **Send Code**.
3. Check your email for a reset code.
4. Enter the code in the app.
5. Enter your new password (at least 6 characters). Confirm it.
6. Tap **Reset Password**. Done! ✅

---

## Logging Out

To log out of LigiOpen:

1. Tap the **Profile tab**.
2. Scroll all the way to the bottom.
3. Tap **Log Out**.
4. Confirm.

> 💡 You'll need to log back in with your email and password next time. Make sure you remember your password before logging out.

>
