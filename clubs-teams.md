# ⚽ Clubs & Teams

Every match in LigiOpen is played between two teams. But before a team can step onto the pitch, their parent club must register, be verified, and officially join a competition. This page explains the complete journey from club registration to match-day readiness, using real Kenyan scenarios and comprehensive examples.

---

## 🏢 Understanding Clubs vs Teams

<div class="info-box">
  <strong>🎯 The Key Distinction</strong><br><br>
  A <strong>CLUB</strong> is the permanent organization — the legal entity with history, colors, a stadium, and management (e.g., <em>AFC Leopards</em>).<br><br>
  A <strong>TEAM</strong> is the squad that a club fields in a specific competition (e.g., <em>AFC Leopards First Team in FKF Premier League</em>, <em>AFC Leopards Reserves in Division One</em>).
</div>

Think of it this way:
- **Club** = The organization itself (founded in 1964, green and white colors, wears the badge)
- **Team** = The specific squad registered to play (first team, reserves, youth team, women's team)

One club can have **multiple teams** competing in different divisions at the same time. This is common in Kenyan football:
- Gor Mahia FC fields their First Team in FKF Premier League
- Gor Mahia FC also registers a Reserves team in National Super League
- Gor Mahia FC runs an U-21 team in youth competitions

Each team is registered separately, has its own squad of players, and competes for different trophies.

<div class="card-grid">
  <div class="card">

  </div>

## 🔄 The Team Registration Lifecycle

<div class="info-box">
  <strong>🔄 Team Registration Status Flow</strong><br><br>
  <strong>PENDING</strong> → Team registration submitted, awaiting league approval<br>
  <strong>APPROVED</strong> → League has approved the registration; team is assigned to division and appears on the official roster<br>
  <strong>CONFIRMED</strong> → Team has met all requirements (squad registered, fees paid) and is fully ready for competition<br>
  <strong>REJECTED</strong> → Registration was denied (club can reapply after resolving issues)
</div>

Let's break down each status:

### Registration Status: PENDING

**What it means:**
- The club has submitted a request to register a team in a specific division
- The league has not yet reviewed or approved it
- The team is **not** on the official roster yet
- The team will **not** be included in fixture generation

**What the club can do:**
- Wait for league approval
- Contact the league admin if approval is delayed
- Cancel the registration request if plans change

**What the league admin can do:**
- Review the request (check club credentials, payment status, eligibility)
- Approve or reject the registration

---

### Registration Status: APPROVED

**What it means:**
- The league has verified the club's eligibility and approved the team to compete
- The team is **officially assigned to the division**
- A **Standing** record is created (team appears on the league table with 0 points)
- The team is now **eligible for fixture generation**

**What the club can do:**
- Register players to the team
- Confirm jersey numbers and squad details
- Prepare for the season

**What the league admin can do:**
- Include the team in fixture generation
- Assign home venues for the team's fixtures

---

### Registration Status: CONFIRMED

**What it means:**
- The team has met all administrative requirements (minimum squad size, registration fees fully paid)
- The team is **fully ready to play matches**
- This is the final "green light" status

**What the club can do:**
- Everything is ready — team can play matches as fixtures are scheduled

**What the league admin can do:**
- Publish fixtures involving this team
- Schedule match dates and venues

---

### Registration Status: REJECTED

**What it means:**
- The league admin denied the registration request
- The team is **not** on the official roster
- The team will **not** compete in the division

**Common rejection reasons:**
- Club has unpaid fees or fines
- Club is under disciplinary ban
- Club submitted incomplete documentation
- Team name conflicts with another registered team
- Division is full (registration deadline passed)

**What happens next:**
- The club receives an email explaining the rejection reason
- The club can resolve the issues (pay fees, submit documents, rename team)
- The club can resubmit the registration once issues are resolved

## 🔄 Managing Multiple Teams from One Club

<div class="scenario">
  <h3>🏆 Scenario: Gor Mahia FC Manages Three Teams</h3>
  
  <p><strong>Gor Mahia FC</strong> is one of Kenya's most successful clubs. They compete at multiple levels:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>First Team</strong> — competes in FKF Premier League</li>
    <li><strong>Reserves</strong> — competes in National Super League (second tier)</li>
    <li><strong>U-21 Team</strong> — competes in FKF Shield (youth tournament)</li>
  </ul>
  
  <p>Let's see how Gor Mahia's administrator manages all three teams in LigiOpen.</p>
</div>

### How Multiple Teams Work

<div class="workflow">
  <div class="workflow-step">
    <div class="step-number">1</div>
    <div class="step-content">
      <h4>One Club Profile, Three Teams</h4>
      <p><strong>Club:</strong> Gor Mahia FC (single club profile)</p>
      
      <p><strong>Teams created under this club:</strong></p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Gor Mahia First Team</strong> → Registered in <em>FKF Premier League → Premier Division</em></li>
        <li><strong>Gor Mahia Reserves</strong> → Registered in <em>National Super League → Division One</em></li>
        <li><strong>Gor Mahia U-21</strong> → Registered in <em>FKF Shield → U-21 Division</em></li>
      </ul>
      
      <p>Each team is treated as a <strong>separate entity</strong> for competition purposes.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="workflow-step">
    <div class="step-number">2</div>
    <div class="step-content">
      <h4>Each Team Has Its Own:</h4>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Squad:</strong> Different players registered to each team</li>
        <li><strong>Fixtures:</strong> First Team plays on Saturdays, Reserves play on Sundays, U-21 plays midweek</li>
        <li><strong>Standings:</strong> Each team has a separate standing record in their respective division</li>
        <li><strong>Match History:</strong> Completely independent match records</li>
      </ul>
      
      <p><em>A player can only be registered to <strong>one team per league per season</strong> (see <a href="players.html">Player Management</a> for eligibility rules).</em></p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="workflow-step">
    <div class="step-number">3</div>
    <div class="step-content">
      <h4>Club Admin Manages All Teams</h4>
      <p>Gor Mahia's Club Admin (e.g., General Manager) can:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Switch between teams from the club dashboard</li>
        <li>Register players to specific teams</li>
        <li>View fixtures for all three teams in one calendar</li>
        <li>Update team logos or details independently</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="info-box">
  <strong>📌 Why Multiple Teams?</strong><br><br>
  <strong>Player Development:</strong> Young players start in U-21, move up to Reserves, then graduate to First Team.<br><br>
  <strong>Competitive Balance:</strong> Reserves teams give bench players match experience without conflicting with first-team fixtures.<br><br>
  <strong>League Requirements:</strong> Some leagues require clubs to field youth teams as part of licensing requirements.
</div>

Clubs and teams can update their profiles at any time.

  </div>

  <div class="card">
    <span class="icon">🎨</span>
    <h3>Visual Identity</h3>
    <p>Club logo, banner image, primary/secondary colors — updated immediately.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="card">
    <span class="icon">🏟️</span>
    <h3>Stadium Details</h3>
    <p>Home stadium name, capacity, directions — informational only; doesn't affect fixture scheduling.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="card">
    <span class="icon">📜</span>
    <h3>Club Description</h3>
    <p>Club history, achievements, mission statement — displayed on the public club profile page.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### What Requires League Approval

<div class="warning-box">
  <strong>⚠️ Changes That Need Approval</strong><br><br>
  <strong>Club Name Change:</strong> Renaming the club requires league admin approval (prevents confusion and potential fraud).<br><br>
  <strong>Team Name Change:</strong> Changing a team's name mid-season requires league approval (to maintain integrity of match records).<br><br>
  <strong>Changing Division:</strong> A team cannot switch divisions mid-season. They must complete the current season and re-register for the next season in the new division.
</div>

## ✅ Club and Team Best Practices

<div class="tip-box">
  <strong>💡 Keep Contact Details Up to Date</strong><br>
  League administrators and referees use your club's email and phone to coordinate matches, send fixture updates, and communicate important information. Always ensure these details are current.
</div>

<div class="tip-box">
  <strong>💡 Use High-Quality Logos</strong><br>
  Your club logo appears on standings tables, fixture lists, team sheets, and social media. Use a clear, professional image (PNG or JPG, minimum 500x500 pixels) for the best presentation.
</div>

<div class="tip-box">
  <strong>💡 Register Early</strong><br>
  Submit your club registration and team applications well before the season start date. Late registrations may be rejected if divisions are full or fixture generation has already begun.
</div>

<div class="tip-box">
  <strong>💡 Assign Multiple Admins for Large Clubs</strong><br>
  If your club has multiple teams or a large squad, assign 2-3 Club Admins to share responsibilities. This ensures someone can always manage urgent tasks (player registrations, injury reports, fixture confirmations).
</div>

<div class="tip-box">
  <strong>💡 Communicate with Your Players</strong><br>
  Once your team is registered, immediately inform your players that they need to claim their LigiOpen profiles. Send them the invitation links so they can activate their accounts and confirm availability for matches.
</div>

## 🏉 Multi-Sport: How Clubs Work Across Different Sports

LigiOpen's club and team structure works identically for all sports — football, rugby, basketball, volleyball, and more.

### Example: Rugby Club with Multiple Teams

<div class="scenario">
  <h3>🏉 Kenya Harlequin RFC</h3>
  
  <p><strong>Club:</strong> Kenya Harlequin RFC</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>First XV</strong> → Registered in <em>Kenya Cup (top-tier rugby)</em></li>
    <li><strong>Second XV</strong> → Registered in <em>Championship (second-tier rugby)</em></li>
    <li><strong>Women's Team</strong> → Registered in <em>Kenya Women's Rugby League</em></li>
  </ul>
  
  <p>The registration process is identical to football:</p>
  <ol>
    <li>Create club profile with rugby-specific details (colors, home ground)</li>
    <li>Get approved by Kenya Rugby Union (KRU)</li>
    <li>Assign club administrators</li>
    <li>Create teams and register them in specific rugby divisions</li>
    <li>Register players (rugby uses squads of 23 instead of 11, but the system accommodates this)</li>
  </ol>
</div>

### Example: Basketball Club

<div class="scenario">
  <h3>🏀 Nairobi City Thunder</h3>
  
  <p><strong>Club:</strong> Nairobi City Thunder Basketball Club</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Men's Team</strong> → Registered in <em>KBF Premier League (men's basketball)</em></li>
    <li><strong>Women's Team</strong> → Registered in <em>KBF Women's League</em></li>
  </ul>
  
  <p><strong>Sport-Specific Differences:</strong></p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Squad size:</strong> Basketball teams typically roster 12-15 players (vs football's 25-30)</li>
    <li><strong>Jersey numbers:</strong> Basketball traditionally uses numbers 4-15 (configurable in LigiOpen)</li>
    <li><strong>Venue:</strong> Indoor court (e.g., Nyayo Gymnasium) instead of outdoor pitch</li>
  </ul>
  
  <p>The club and team registration process remains exactly the same.</p>
</div>

<div class="info-box">
  <strong>🌍 Universal Club Structure</strong><br><br>
  Whether you're managing a football club in Mombasa, a rugby club in Nakuru, or a basketball club in Kisumu, the club and team registration workflow is consistent across all sports. This makes LigiOpen easy to learn and use regardless of the sport.
</div>
